# Ticket Fairy

> Ticket Fairy is a ticketing, marketing and operations platform for live events — festivals, club nights, concerts, conferences and tours. Organisers use it to sell tickets, build event websites, run referral and paid marketing, handle door operations, and get paid; it also offers embedded banking and working capital financed against ticket sales. It has processed over $300M in ticket sales across the US, UK, Canada, Mexico, the EU, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Indonesia, India and other markets, for events from 50 to 100,000+ attendees, and is backed by Y Combinator.

## What Ticket Fairy does

- [Ticketing and checkout](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing): Sell tickets for festivals, club nights, concerts, conferences and tours.
- [Embedded checkout](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/embedded-ticket-checkout): Sell on the organiser's own website instead of sending fans elsewhere.
- [Event website builder](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/event-website-builder): Build and publish an event or brand site without a developer.
- [Referral marketing](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/referral-marketing-ticketing): Reward the fans who bring other fans, tracked to the individual sale.
- [Multi-network advertising](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/multi-network-event-advertising): Plan, launch and measure paid campaigns across ad networks from one place.
- [Social media management](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/social-media-management): Publish, schedule and reply across the brand's social accounts.
- [Abandoned cart recovery](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/abandoned-cart-recovery): Bring back buyers who left before paying.
- [AI concierge and support inbox](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/ai-event-concierge): An AI agent that answers fans, and one inbox for every channel.
- [Analytics and reporting](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/event-data-analytics): Where sales come from, what they cost, and what to do next.
- [Door scanning and check-in](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/ticket-scanning-app): Scan tickets at the door, online or offline.
- [RFID and cashless](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/rfid-cashless-events): Wristband access control and cashless spending on site.
- [Anti-scalping and face-value resale](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/anti-scalping-ticketing): A resale marketplace capped at face value so tickets stay with fans.
- [Payment plans](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/event-payment-plans): Let buyers pay for a ticket in instalments.
- [White-label](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/white-label-ticketing): Run the entire platform under a partner's own brand and domain.
- [Event banking (Vault)](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/event-banking): Multi-currency accounts and corporate cards for event businesses.
- [Event funding (Capital)](https://www.ticketfairy.com/capital): Working capital advanced against ticket sales.
- [Fai — the AI co-pilot](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/ticket-fairy-ai): An AI agent that can run the dashboard alongside the organiser.

- [Promoters and agencies](https://www.ticketfairy.com/event-ticketing/promoter-ticketing-management-platform): Many events across many brands, with roles for each team.
